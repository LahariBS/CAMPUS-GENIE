Admission Process

Follow these steps to complete your college admission process.

1. Choose Your College and Course

Select the college and course that matches your interests, career goals and academic qualifications.

2. Check Eligibility

Check the educational qualifications, minimum marks, entrance examination requirements and other eligibility criteria.

3. Prepare Documents

Keep all required academic certificates, identity documents, photographs and other supporting documents ready.

4. Submit Application

Complete the application form carefully and submit it through the appropriate admission portal or college website.

5. Complete Verification

Complete document verification, counselling or entrance-related procedures when required.

6. Confirm Admission

After receiving admission confirmation, complete the required fee payment and registration process.
