How to Apply

Follow these steps to apply for internships and prepare for the selection process.

1. Find a Suitable Internship

Choose an internship that matches your interests, educational background and skills.

2. Check Eligibility

Read the internship requirements carefully, including qualifications, skills and duration.

3. Prepare Your Resume

Create an updated resume highlighting your education, technical skills, projects, certifications and achievements.

4. Prepare Your Projects

Keep your important academic and personal projects available on GitHub or another suitable portfolio platform.

5. Submit Your Application

Apply through the company's official careers page or a trusted internship platform.

6. Prepare for the Interview

Revise programming fundamentals, technical concepts, projects and common HR interview questions.
