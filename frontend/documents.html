Required Documents

Common documents that may be required during the admission process.

📄 Academic Documents

• 10th marks card

• 12th or diploma marks card

• Transfer Certificate

• Entrance examination scorecard, if applicable

🪪 Identity Documents

• Government-issued identity proof

• Passport-size photographs

📑 Supporting Certificates

• Caste/category certificate, if applicable

• Income certificate, if applicable

• Domicile/residence certificate, if applicable

⚠️ Important

The required documents can vary depending on the college, course and admission category.

Check the official admission notification before submitting your application.
